## Move 01 · The stockpile

Every pre-save is a stream that has already happened, it just has not been counted yet.

That is the entire trick, and it is why the pre-save wall matters more than any single piece of release-week promotion. By the time petal went live, 3.22 million plays were effectively pre-loaded into the first hours. No advertising bought that. It was collected over weeks, in advance, from people who had already decided.

For an independent artist the arithmetic is smaller but identical. 200 pre-saves means 200 plays land in hour one instead of trickling across a fortnight, and hour one is when the systems are watching.

## Move 02 · The payoff is velocity, not volume

62.19 million streams on day one is a volume number. The reason all 12 tracks charted inside 24 hours is a velocity number.

Recommendation systems weight rate of change heavily, because rate of change is much harder to fake than a total. A release that gathers 10,000 streams in its first six hours reads as a live event. The same 10,000 spread across three weeks reads as background.

Pre-saves are the only reliable way an artist without a marketing budget can compress plays into that first window on purpose.

## Move 03 · Your version of the same machine

Scale it down and nothing about the logic changes.

**Open the pre-save at announcement, not at release week.** Every day the link is live is a day it collects. Most independents launch it three days out and lose the majority of the window.

**Ask directly and repeatedly.** Pre-saving is an unfamiliar action for casual listeners, so the ask needs to be explicit: what it is, that it takes ten seconds, and that the song simply appears in their library on release day.

**Put the link where the intent is.** Bio, Stories with a link sticker, pinned comment, email. Not a single feed post that disappears in four hours.

**Do not buy them.** Purchased pre-saves come from accounts that never listen again, and a spike with no second-day retention is a worse signal than no spike at all.

## What does not transfer

Everything above the mechanism. Ariana Grande entered release day with a fanbase built across a decade, a press cycle that runs itself and a film franchise in the same window. The pre-save wall converted attention that already existed.

That is the honest limit of any major-label case study. The tactic is portable, the demand is not. Build the demand first, then use the mechanism to compress it into a single day.
