The European Commission's AI Act transparency obligations took effect on 2 August 2026. Content that is generated or meaningfully edited by AI and distributed in the EU must be labeled, and the marking has to be machine-readable rather than a line in a caption.

For working musicians this cuts two ways. If you use generative tools, for artwork, video, or elements of a track, disclosure is now a legal requirement in the EU rather than an ethical preference. If you do not, the same rules are the mechanism that makes voice cloning traceable, which is the first real protection artists have been given on that front.

Platforms moved quickly behind it. Spotify announced its AI Persona badges nine days later, on 11 August. That sequence is worth noting: regulation set the deadline, and the platforms shipped inside two weeks.
